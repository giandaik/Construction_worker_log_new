# Hero image — Sitely Construction Log landing page

## Visual brief

Photorealistic editorial photography, no text in the image. A construction site at golden hour: a concrete building under construction, exposed rebar and formwork, warm afternoon light raking across the structure. In the foreground, a worker in a safety vest and hard hat holds a smartphone at a slight angle, camera app open and pointed at the site, clearly about to capture a progress photo — candid, not posed, dusty gloves, sleeves rolled up. The phone screen glows subtly.

Palette must match the app design system: warm paper-white concrete and dust tones, charcoal/dark slate shadows, and one strong safety-yellow accent (vest, hard hat band, or site barrier) — industrial, high contrast, bright daylight feel. Shallow depth of field, natural color grading, no heavy filter, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Composition

Wide cinematic 16:9 composition. Phone occupies lower-left third; the building fills the upper two-thirds. Leaves clear negative space in the upper-right for headline text overlay.
